The two PNG diagrams supply the domain labels. The SVG supplies a simplified quadrant framework with names, elements, actions, and orientation labels.

Top: **Outside**. Bottom: **Inside**.
Left: **Constructive / Cold**. Right: **Creative / Warm**.

These relationships are preserved as labelled. The source does not define a measurement unit or a mapping from content to numerical coordinates. The four ACID lenses occupy quadrants in a diagram with two directional dimensions; do not turn that geometry into four independent measured axes without describing the adaptation.

The Origin image includes elemental, zodiac, and yin-yang imagery. Those symbols provide visual context. The source materials supplied here do not establish empirical validation, personality classification, or astrological prediction.

## Original materials

The repository's `artwork/` directory contains unchanged copies of the supplied files:

- `acid-origin.png`: originally `ACID Mehtod - Origin.png` (original spelling).

The installable skill bundles the original framework SVG at `assets/ACID Method - Framework.svg` for the required heat-map visual. The other original artwork remains in the repository for human reference.

## Heat-map adaptation in version 0.1.1

Each use includes a qualitative heat-map overlay on the original framework SVG. The overlay communicates relative emphasis inferred from the supplied evidence and distinguishes unknown areas from low emphasis. It is a presentation adaptation requested by the user, not part of the original diagrams or a validated scoring method. The original artwork remains unchanged beneath the generated overlay.

## What version 0.1.0 adds

The evidence-based analysis workflow, guiding questions, output suggestions, examples, and evaluation cases are proposed operational guidance for applying these diagrams in an LLM. They are not transcriptions of a longer ACID manual. The original source consists of the three diagrams above.

No numerical scoring rubric, fixed weighting, ideal balance, or validated diagnostic instrument is supplied. Future additions should identify their source or state explicitly that they are adaptations.
